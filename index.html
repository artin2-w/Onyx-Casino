What is Onyx Casino?

Onyx Casino is a browser-based casino simulator built around fictional virtual credits, local progression, missions, and playable games.

Virtual credits only

Credits are play money stored in your browser save. They cannot become anything outside the simulator.

Balance and VIP

Playing games wagers credits, awards XP, and moves your VIP progress. Higher tiers improve simulated reward amounts and table limits.

Penthouse and status

The Onyx Penthouse lets you buy fictional in-game assets, grow net worth, equip a showcase, and collect browser-local passive income.

Missions and rewards

Daily-style missions, achievements, promo-style codes, and the reward calendar grant virtual credits and XP only.

Playable games

Slots use paylines, scatters, and free spins. Roulette supports one active bet type at a time. Blackjack uses a six-deck shoe. Dice, Mines, Crash, and Plinko use risk-based simulator multipliers.

Troubleshooting
